# A little love, in bloom

Direction from the brief: a romantic birthday website in soft pink pastel with flowers, ribbon, a gift reveal, a phone-like password screen, a scrolling memory history, and a light sakura shower.

Reading this as: an interactive birthday letter for someone loved, in a soft editorial scrapbook style. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

- Color: dusty rose and warm cream create the requested pastel romance; deep berry ink keeps the writing legible. Muted sage is used only for botanical details.
- Layout: lock screen, gift reveal, memories, then a letter follow the order of the surprise itself rather than a landing-page template.
- Typography: a literary serif for intimate headings, paired with a readable sans for controls and longer notes.
- Spacing: the reveal gets breathing room; the story section tightens into an alternating scrapbook rhythm.
- Photo frames: visible, explicitly labeled placeholders preserve space for real shared photos without inventing them. On opening the gift, a rain of falling photo frames reuses the photos configured for each memory.
- Illustration: the bouquet rises from the box; a folded-paper heart seal on its front makes the requested love symbol look like an attached handmade detail.
- Motion: an on-demand ribbon burst flies outward before the bouquet rises and memory photos keep falling from above until the page closes, as explicitly requested; a small number of sakura petals fall slowly. Reduced-motion users get an immediate reveal.
- Music: a local instrumental WAV starts from the gift click, avoiding fragile real-time synthesizer playback and network dependencies.
- Elevation: only the phone, gift, and paper-like photo frames have depth, to make them feel touchable.
