# A little love, in bloom

Direction from the brief: a romantic birthday website in soft pink pastel with flowers, ribbon, a gift reveal, a phone-like password screen, a scrolling memory history, and a light sakura shower.

Reading this as: an interactive birthday letter for someone loved, in a soft editorial scrapbook style. The visit moves from the password to an October calendar, a birthday greeting, and the existing flower-and-memory surprise. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

- Color: dusty rose and warm cream create the requested pastel romance; deep berry ink keeps the writing legible. Muted sage is used only for botanical details.
- Layout: lock screen, gift reveal, memories, then a letter follow the order of the surprise itself rather than a landing-page template.
- Typography: a literary serif for intimate headings, paired with a readable sans for controls and longer notes.
- Spacing: the reveal gets breathing room; ten short, alternating scrapbook entries each pair one sentence with one framed photo.
- Photo frames: the ten memory sentences pair with the ten photos from `solo/`; the album uses all ten curated images in `album/` plus the photo moved from `cover/`.
- Photo album: a hardcover book with a spine, page edges, a ribbon bookmark, and a single leaf that turns around the gutter, because the request asked for a real school book, not a card grid; the cover carries the title "Album Kita" and the date so it reads as a keepsake.
- Album opening: the remaining photo from `cover/` and five short greeting pages appear after the hardcover opens and before the main photo pages.
- Album pages: a 3x3 grid of framed photos with tiny captions and a page number; all ten photos in `album/` and the moved opening photo are included.
- Responsive album: keep the same open, two-page landscape spread and 3x3 photo grid on phones and larger screens; scale the hardcover to fit the available width instead of changing its format.
- Album palette: the cover is soft pastel rose with deep berry ink for the title, and the pages stay warm cream, so the book belongs to the pastel romance; the spine and back cover use the same pastel rose family.
- Album motion: one leaf turns in 0.9s with an ease-out curve while a gutter shadow fades in, because the request wants a book feel; the leaf is promoted to its own layer and the next page is prefetched and decoded in advance, so the turn stays smooth; reduced-motion users get an instant page change.
- Illustration: the bouquet rises from the box; a folded-paper heart seal on its front makes the requested love symbol look like an attached handmade detail.
- Motion: an on-demand ribbon burst flies outward before the bouquet rises. A small number of sakura petals fall slowly. Reduced-motion users get an immediate reveal.
- Web photos: every personal photo in `solo/`, `album/`, and `cover/` is included with a visible frame.
- Music: the user-provided, authorized Love Songs MP3 plays locally; the header control lets the listener pause and resume without sending them away from the birthday page.
- Elevation: only the phone, gift, and paper-like photo frames have depth, to make them feel touchable.
